How to read the result

Three things worth knowing.

  • 1Blanks are fine. Missing numbers become the training median and missing categories the most frequent value — so you never have to guess a number you do not know.
  • 2The percentage is confidence in “placed”. Around 50% means the model is genuinely unsure about that student.
  • 3It is a classroom model. Useful for understanding the data, not a real admissions or hiring forecast.
